## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Zibda is a calorie and macro tracker built for the Libyan market, the food
global calorie apps don't recognize.

Local packaged brands and everyday ingredients, searchable in Arabic or
English, sit alongside everyday portions like a plate, bowl, cup, or glass,
so you're not stuck guessing gram weights.

What makes Zibda different:

- Works completely offline. No internet connection needed, ever.
- No account, no sign-up. Nothing to lose access to.
- No ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.
- Full Arabic support with right-to-left layout and a one-tap language
  toggle.
- Nutrition data goes through a real verification pipeline against product
  labels, and verified entries are marked as such, not presented as
  guesses.

Log meals against your own calorie and macro goals, build saved meals for
things you eat often, and see your history at a glance.

Zibda records what you eat. It does not diagnose or prescribe, and it is
not a substitute for medical advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian for
anything beyond tracking.
